Mind2Act
World:
Evaluating Reasoning–Acting Coordination
in Robotic Manipulation
Demo video · media placeholder
Toward general-purpose embodied intelligence, robots must execute long-horizon tasks in complex, dynamic environments, combine high-level task organization with physical interaction, and continually adapt their decisions as the environment changes. However, existing robotic manipulation benchmarks often focus on isolated atomic skills or short-horizon objectives in simplified scenes. Such settings are useful for evaluating primitive control and conducting reproducible experiments, but offer limited evidence of whether robots can maintain task state throughout a multi-stage manipulation process and effectively couple reasoning with physical execution.
To address this gap, we introduce
Mind2Act World
, a benchmark that systematically evaluates robotic task reasoning, physical execution, and their coordination through contextualized tasks, tiered stress configurations, and process-level diagnostics. Mind2Act World comprises 15 tasks across three complementary dimensions:
Mind, Act, and Mind2Act
.
Mind
measures the ability to organize actions at the task-cognition level, including maintaining task-relevant state, interpreting instructions and observations, inferring goals and prerequisites, and determining an appropriate sequence of operations.
Act
measures the ability to realize intended operations in the physical world through timely, precise, and stable interactions under timing, geometric, contact, and stability constraints.
Mind2Act
evaluates whether reasoning and execution can be jointly realized through ongoing interaction: task intent must guide physically valid actions, while actual execution outcomes must inform remaining goals, action timing, and phase progression. The central question is whether a robot can maintain both task-level correctness and effective physical execution while keeping subsequent decisions consistent with what has actually been completed.
Overview of Mind2Act World
The task suite embeds primitive manipulation skills in multi-stage contexts with practical task backgrounds, persistent state, and explicit phase dependencies, with tiered stress configurations designed around each task's mechanisms. Each task has
Easy, Medium, and Hard
levels that vary historical information load, planning and phase dependencies, precision and contact requirements, and dynamic response windows to examine capability boundaries under different pressures. Evaluation combines task success rates with process evidence on goal selection, physical execution outcomes, and phase progression to analyze whether failures primarily reflect task understanding and planning deficiencies in
Mind
, physical execution deficiencies in
Act
, or difficulties coordinating reasoning and execution in
Mind2Act
. Through stratified results, process records, and applicable controlled comparisons, the benchmark aims to reveal the conditions under which models fail and how local deviations affect subsequent decisions and propagate through multi-stage manipulation.
To support reproducible and interpretable comparisons across
VLA/WAM policies, Coding Agents, and hybrid systems
, Mind2Act World specifies observation and action interfaces, timing conditions, and disclosure requirements for execution assistance, and determines task completion from actual environment-side physical states. By combining contextualized task design, tiered stress evaluation, and closed-loop process diagnostics, Mind2Act World provides a systematic framework for studying reasoning, execution, and their coordination in robotic manipulation.

The current release includes 15 task specifications, Easy/Medium/Hard configurations, demonstration media, and an evaluation protocol. Model scores and leaderboard results are not yet reported.

COMPANION METHOD / ASTRA–JEV PROPOSAL
Mind2Act Harness
Contract-Guided Coordination of Cognitive and Reactive Agents for Robotic Manipulation
新版方案聚焦成本感知的 Astra–Jev 协同：GPT-6 Astra 拆解子任务并调用感知工具，harness 将结果、候选与工作记忆编译为结构化文本，Jev 选择当前适用候选，由 Runtime 核验后执行。
Evidence-Grounded State
交接保留观测来源、坐标系、时效和未知项，区分动作回执与实际效果。
Candidate-Constrained Jev
Jev 选择绑定完整动作的候选 ID；连续参数、授权和失效检查由 harness 处理。
Budgeted Memory & Routing
保留任务关键事实，按事件局部刷新或升级 Astra，计入所有感知、摘要、重试与执行成本。
方法状态以最新 Harness 页面为准：已有外部 Case7 开发原型，RSI 与正式 benchmark 的收益仍待验证。此处 case 更新不产生新的模型成绩或成本结论。

完成条件与评测指标
完成本档准备流程后，按示范顺序和次数有效复奏，归还所用敲击棒并撤离。Medium/Hard 需要双臂揭罩与同步取放双棒，Hard 还需将音量推杆设至 MAX；执行不按示范节拍评分。具体触发、释放与稳定归位阈值应与环境判据对齐。

参数与评测说明
Easy 为 7 键/8 次/单臂单棒；Medium 为 14 键/8 次/双臂双棒；Hard 为 21 键/12 次/双臂双棒。
示范每两秒一个事件，执行不要求跟拍。双棒按序轮流敲击，取放同步；不能将同步取放等同于同步击键。
准备流程使用防尘布，取代旧版琴盖开合；收尾为归还敲击棒并撤离，不要求恢复琴盖或盖回防尘布。
三段视频按来源标注分别为 Easy 63 秒、Medium 97 秒、Hard 129 秒；播放时长由本地媒体读取。演示不构成被测模型成绩。
