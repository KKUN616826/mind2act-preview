Act · FR2

Light-Guided Button Response

Respond to illuminated button cues with timely, correct presses and releases.

Evaluation dimensions

Cue response time; button selection; press and release control.

Easy · Medium · Hard

Easy

Press the currently illuminated button within 4.0 seconds and release within 1 second. Respond correctly to all 10 cues; never press an unlit button.

Medium

Press the currently illuminated button within 3.2 seconds and release within 1 second. Respond correctly to all 10 cues; never press an unlit button.

Hard

Press the currently illuminated button within 2.6 seconds and release within 1 second. Respond correctly to all 10 cues; never press an unlit button.
