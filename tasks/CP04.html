Mind2Act · CP04

Dual-Arm Cooking

Remember a recipe and coordinate preparation, cooking, temperature control, and cleanup.

Evaluation dimensions

Recipe sequence memory; dual-arm coordination; temperature feedback and phase progression.

Easy · Medium · Hard

Easy

Observe the complete recipe, remember its order, then cook it. Return each empty jar upright, place and release the pot lid, heat, keep warm, and turn off. Switch HEAT to KEEP WARM within 4 seconds after reaching80C (green80–90C); physical OFF freezes temperature.

Medium

Observe the complete recipe, remember its order, then cook it. Return each empty jar upright, place and release the pot lid, heat, keep warm, and turn off. Switch HEAT to KEEP WARM within 3 seconds after reaching80C (green80–87.5C); physical OFF freezes temperature.

Hard

Observe the complete recipe, remember its order, then cook it. Return each empty jar upright, place and release the pot lid, heat, keep warm, and turn off. Switch HEAT to KEEP WARM within 2 seconds after reaching80C (green80–85C); physical OFF freezes temperature.
