Mind · F

Unknown Button Mapping and Slider Navigation

Infer an unknown button-to-motion mapping from feedback, retain it after reset, and navigate to the target.

Evaluation dimensions

Feedback-based rule inference; mapping retention; route planning.

Easy · Medium · Hard

Easy

Probe both unmarked buttons and remember how each moves the slider. After the slider resets and the goal appears, reach the visible goal using the fewest presses.

Medium

Probe four identical unmarked buttons distinguished only by spatial position. Infer the unknown cardinal-direction mapping through visible movement. After reset, preserve the mapping and navigate to the visible goal on the 3 by 3 grid using a shortest route of 2–3 steps.

Hard

Probe four identical unmarked buttons distinguished only by spatial position. Infer the unknown cardinal-direction mapping through visible movement. After reset, preserve the mapping and navigate to the visible goal on the 4 by 4 grid using a shortest route. Both axes change; shortest distance is 4–6.
