Act · PR1

Linear Resistor Adjustment

Adjust a linear resistor to the target reading and maintain it after release.

Evaluation dimensions

Position precision; resistance adjustment; stability after release.

Easy · Medium · Hard

Easy

Adjust the resistance between the left terminal and wiper to 35 ohm (±0.2 ohm). Release and hold for 1 second.

Medium

Adjust the resistance between the left terminal and wiper to 10.5 ohm (±0.2 ohm). Release and hold for 1 second.

Hard

Adjust the resistance between the left terminal and wiper to 26.25 ohm (±0.2 ohm). Release and hold for 1 second.
