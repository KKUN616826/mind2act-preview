Mind2Act World:Evaluating Reasoning–Acting Coordinationin Robotic Manipulation

Toward general-purpose embodied intelligence, robots must execute long-horizon tasks in complex, dynamic environments, combine high-level task organization with physical interaction, and continually adapt their decisions as the environment changes. However, existing robotic manipulation benchmarks often focus on isolated atomic skills or short-horizon objectives in simplified scenes. Such settings are useful for evaluating primitive control and conducting reproducible experiments, but offer limited evidence of whether robots can maintain task state throughout a multi-stage manipulation process and effectively couple reasoning with physical execution.

To address this gap, we introduce Mind2Act World, a benchmark that systematically evaluates robotic task reasoning, physical execution, and their coordination through contextualized tasks, tiered stress configurations, and process-level diagnostics. Mind2Act World comprises 15 tasks across three complementary dimensions: Mind, Act, and Mind2Act. Mind measures the ability to organize actions at the task-cognition level, including maintaining task-relevant state, interpreting instructions and observations, inferring goals and prerequisites, and determining an appropriate sequence of operations. Act measures the ability to realize intended operations in the physical world through timely, precise, and stable interactions under timing, geometric, contact, and stability constraints. Mind2Act evaluates whether reasoning and execution can be jointly realized through ongoing interaction: task intent must guide physically valid actions, while actual execution outcomes must inform remaining goals, action timing, and phase progression. The central question is whether a robot can maintain both task-level correctness and effective physical execution while keeping subsequent decisions consistent with what has actually been completed.

The task suite embeds primitive manipulation skills in multi-stage contexts with practical task backgrounds, persistent state, and explicit phase dependencies, with tiered stress configurations designed around each task's mechanisms. Each task has Easy, Medium, and Hard levels that vary historical information load, planning and phase dependencies, precision and contact requirements, and dynamic response windows to examine capability boundaries under different pressures. Evaluation combines task success rates with process evidence on goal selection, physical execution outcomes, and phase progression to analyze whether failures primarily reflect task understanding and planning deficiencies in Mind, physical execution deficiencies in Act, or difficulties coordinating reasoning and execution in Mind2Act. Through stratified results, process records, and applicable controlled comparisons, the benchmark aims to reveal the conditions under which models fail and how local deviations affect subsequent decisions and propagate through multi-stage manipulation.

To support reproducible and interpretable comparisons across VLA/WAM policies, Coding Agents, and hybrid systems, Mind2Act World specifies observation and action interfaces, timing conditions, and disclosure requirements for execution assistance, and determines task completion from actual environment-side physical states. By combining contextualized task design, tiered stress evaluation, and closed-loop process diagnostics, Mind2Act World provides a systematic framework for studying reasoning, execution, and their coordination in robotic manipulation.

A benchmark for closed-loop robotic manipulation

15 tasks across Mind, Act, and Mind2Act. Each task has Easy, Medium, and Hard configurations.

Mind

Memory-driven task decisions: entity, feedback, process, spatial, and integrated memory.

E Duplicate Item Identification

Identify previously observed items across successive batches and place each repeated item in the submission tray.

Evaluation dimensions

Entity memory; cross-batch retrieval; target selection and physical submission.

F Unknown Button Mapping and Slider Navigation

Infer an unknown button-to-motion mapping from feedback, retain it after reset, and navigate to the target.

Evaluation dimensions

Feedback-based rule inference; mapping retention; route planning.

P Order-Based Meal Assembly

Remember initial and additional orders and deliver the requested ingredients to the correct containers.

Evaluation dimensions

Order memory; accumulated demand tracking; correct ingredient delivery.

S Rotating Tray Layout Reconstruction

Remember a colored-slot arrangement and restore it after the tray rotates.

Evaluation dimensions

Spatial memory; rotation tracking; matching placement.

M Workpiece Tracking and Route Reproduction

Track a hidden target through cover exchanges and reproduce its required station route.

Evaluation dimensions

Target tracking; route memory; ordered station completion.

Act

Dynamic response, precision, contact, and stable physical execution.

FR1 Continuous Conveyor Picking

Pick moving packages from a conveyor and place them in the collection tray.

Evaluation dimensions

Dynamic target interception; grasp timing; stable deposit.

FR2 Light-Guided Button Response

Respond to illuminated button cues with timely, correct presses and releases.

Evaluation dimensions

Cue response time; button selection; press and release control.

PR1 Linear Resistor Adjustment

Adjust a linear resistor to the target reading and maintain it after release.

Evaluation dimensions

Position precision; resistance adjustment; stability after release.

PR2 Rotary Voltage Regulator Setting

Adjust a rotary voltage regulator to the target reading and maintain it after release.

Evaluation dimensions

Rotational precision; voltage adjustment; stability after release.

PR3 Marker Line Tracing

Grasp a marker and trace a visible path with controlled contact and precision.

Evaluation dimensions

Path accuracy; tool contact; tracing continuity.

Mind2Act

Reasoning and execution coordinated across multi-stage tasks.

CP01 Conveyor Meal Assembly

Remember reference orders and assemble meals from a continuously moving food supply.

Evaluation dimensions

Order memory; moving-target grasping; meal completion.

CP02 Rotating Cake Decoration

Remember a decoration layout and reproduce it on a rotating cake while completing the required stages.

Evaluation dimensions

Arrangement memory; rotating-target placement; stage prerequisites.

CP03 Dynamic Rack Loading

Load upright plates into moving empty rack slots without disturbing other plates.

Evaluation dimensions

Moving-slot timing; upright insertion; contact stability.

CP04 Dual-Arm Cooking

Remember a recipe and coordinate preparation, cooking, temperature control, and cleanup.

Evaluation dimensions

Recipe sequence memory; dual-arm coordination; temperature feedback and phase progression.

CP05 Keyboard Sequence Reproduction

Remember a demonstrated key sequence, reproduce it with mallets, and return the tools.

Evaluation dimensions

Key sequence memory; precise key contact and release; tool handling and preparation.
