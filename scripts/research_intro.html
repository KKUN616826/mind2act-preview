Robotic manipulation benchmarks often evaluate perception and motion execution separately, leaving the interaction between task reasoning and physical execution underexplored. Mind2Act World addresses this gap with 15 simulation tasks that evaluate whether an agent can identify task-relevant goals, act under physical constraints, verify the resulting state, and adapt subsequent actions accordingly.

BENCHMARK

A benchmark for closed-loop robotic manipulation

Mind2Act World comprises 15 simulation tasks organized into three capability suites and three difficulty levels. Each task evaluates whether an agent can preserve task context, execute under motion and contact constraints, verify physical outcomes, and adapt its subsequent actions.

EVALUATION

From decision to physical outcome

Each rollout is evaluated using environment-derived evidence. The protocol separately measures goal selection, physical task completion, and whether subsequent actions respond to observed outcomes.
