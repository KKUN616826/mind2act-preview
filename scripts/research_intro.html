Robotic manipulation benchmarks often evaluate what a system recognizes or how accurately it executes a prescribed motion, while leaving the interaction between reasoning and physical execution under-specified. To address this limitation, we introduce Mind2Act World, a simulation benchmark of 15 manipulation tasks designed to measure how agents identify task-relevant goals, act under physical constraints, and update subsequent decisions from observed outcomes.

BENCHMARK

A benchmark for complete robotic tasks

We design 15 simulation tasks across three capability suites and three difficulty levels. The benchmark tests whether an agent can retain task context, satisfy motion and contact constraints, verify physical outcomes, and use those outcomes to choose the next action.

EVALUATION

From decision to physical outcome

Every run is evaluated against environment-side evidence. The protocol separates choosing the right goal, physically completing it, and using the observed result to plan the next step.

REASON→ACT→OBSERVE→UPDATE
01

Goal selection

Does the agent choose the object, location, and operation required by the task?

02

Physical completion

Does the environment confirm grasping, placement, release, contact, and stability?

03

Outcome-conditioned update

Does the next decision reflect what actually happened in the environment?
